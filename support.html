Getting started

Forge needs a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on (System Settings ▸ Apple Intelligence & Siri). Open Forge, explore any page, then subscribe to Forge Pro to start building.

Forge Pro subscription

  • Restore: open the paywall and choose Restore Purchases, or Settings ▸ Account ▸ Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel or change: Settings ▸ Account ▸ Manage Subscription, or your Apple Account’s Subscriptions page.
  • Refunds: requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

“Forge Needs Apple Intelligence.” Turn on Apple Intelligence and wait for the model to finish downloading.

Terminal access. Forge asks once before running commands. Change it any time in Settings ▸ Terminal.

Design Studio. Use New Page to add pages, Preview to click through your prototype, and My Apps to save and reopen designs.

Private Cloud. Larger requests may use Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, which has a daily limit; Forge falls back to the on-device model automatically.

Contact

Need help or found a bug? Send us a support request (a free GitHub account is needed) and we’ll get back to you. Please don’t include passwords, API keys or other private details, as requests are public.
