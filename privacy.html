Forge Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

ForgeSupportPrivacy

Forge is designed to keep your work private. We don’t run servers that store your data, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you. This policy explains what Forge does with information on your device.

Information we collect

We (the developer of Forge) do not collect, receive, or store your personal data. Everything below stays on your device, in your own iCloud account, or goes directly to a service you choose.

Data that stays on your device and in your iCloud

  • Your conversations, Master Plans, designs (My Apps), memory notes, and settings are stored on your Mac.
  • If you’re signed in to iCloud, some settings, plans, and memory notes sync through your own private iCloud key-value storage so they’re available on your other devices. We can’t access your iCloud data.
  • API keys you add for AI providers are stored in your Keychain.

Sign in with Apple

When you sign in with Apple, Apple gives Forge an anonymous account identifier and, the first time, the name and email you choose to share (which may be a private relay address). Forge keeps these on your device and in your iCloud only, to link your Forge Pro subscription to your account. You can sign out in Settings ▸ Account, which removes them from the device.

AI processing

  • Apple Intelligence: requests are handled by Apple’s on-device model or Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designs so that your data isn’t stored or made accessible to anyone, including Apple. See Apple’s privacy information for details.
  • Your own AI providers (optional): if you add a provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or others in Providers, the prompts, files, and images you send while that provider is selected go directly from your Mac to that provider under their privacy policy. Forge never sends this data to us.
  • Your own MLX server (optional): requests go only to the server address you enter.

Web search

If Web Search is on, the search terms Forge needs go to DuckDuckGo to fetch results. No account or identifier is sent. You can turn Web Search off in Settings.

Plugins

MCP plugins you install run on your Mac and may connect to the services they’re built for (for example GitHub or Notion) using credentials you provide. Their handling of data is governed by those services.

Permissions

Forge asks before using the microphone and speech recognition (voice conversations, transcribed on device), Calendars and Reminders, Contacts (only when you ask about a contact), and files or folders you choose. Forge asks once before running Terminal commands. You can change any of these in System Settings or Forge Settings.

Purchases

Forge Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We receive only Apple’s standard anonymous transaction records needed to unlock Forge Pro; we never see your payment details.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Forge contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Forge is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can delete chats, plans, and designs inside Forge at any time, sign out in Settings ▸ Account, and remove all Forge data by deleting the app. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions? Visit Forge Support.
